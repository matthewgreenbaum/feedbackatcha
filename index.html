About FeedbackAtcha

What is FeedbackAtcha?

FeedbackAtcha is an AI-powered tool that helps B2B SaaS marketers craft professional, actionable creative feedback. Think of it as the experienced colleague who always knows exactly what to say in a creative review.

Who is this for?

Marketing managers, directors, and VPs who review creative work regularly — ads, emails, landing pages, decks, videos, and more. Whether you're giving feedback to designers, copywriters, agencies, or cross-functional teams.

How does it work?

Paste your copy, upload a creative asset, or just share your raw impressions. Select the asset type and context, and FeedbackAtcha generates structured, professional feedback using proven frameworks like I Like / I Wish / What If, SBI, and COIN.

What are the three modes?

Full Read gives you a situation assessment plus a complete feedback draft. Quick Take is a sharp one-liner followed by the draft. Just Draft It skips the preamble and goes straight to polished feedback.

What about my brand guidelines?

Open the Brand Profile panel to add your website URL, brand guidelines (paste text, upload a PDF, or link to a doc), voice and tone notes, and brand colors. When enabled, your brand context is woven into every piece of feedback.

Is my data private?

FeedbackAtcha runs entirely in your browser. Your API key and brand settings are stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server other than the Anthropic API to generate feedback.

Do I need an API key?

Yes. FeedbackAtcha uses the Anthropic API (Claude) to generate feedback. You'll need to paste your API key into the code. Your key is never stored on any external server.
